# Anti Slop Audit 001 - 2026-10-11

Scope: cashier petty cash dashboard, shift opening, cash expense UI, and cash management page.
Design direction: `DESIGN.md` (quiet monochrome workspace, mobile-first, direct operational labels).

## Findings

1. **HIGH - R-35 (Verify Before You Deliver)**
   The cashier dashboard now adds a petty cash metric and may add a debt metric to the existing responsive metric grid (`resources/views/dashboard/role.blade.php`), but no browser run or mobile-width check has been recorded for the changed workflow. Bootstrap breakpoints alone do not verify that the amount remains easy to find on a narrow screen.

2. **LOW - R-05 (Layout & Page Structure)**
   The cashier cash management page combines handover requests, shift cash movements, a warehouse cash ledger, and a finance bank ledger in one long view (`resources/views/livewire/finance/cash-management-index.blade.php:5-24`); the bank ledger section is rendered even when the component supplies no rows because the user lacks bank-account access. The page mixes separate tasks and adds an empty finance table to the cashier's view.

## No other findings

The shift form no longer asks for opening cash and explains the automatic Rp50.000 initial amount and carried balance. The expense form clearly identifies cashier expenses as cash from the active petty cash/shift. These changes match the established operational direction; no other specific Anti Slop rule violation was evidenced in the reviewed scope.
