# Design direction

## Identity

The workspace is an independent operational interface for DimsumWeigu. It should feel professional and quiet, with no Mazer Admin visual identity.

## Audience

Cashiers, outlet staff, and managers who use the workspace on phones as well as desktop screens.

## Personality

Simple, modern, monochrome, and practical. Keep dense operational data calm and easy to scan. Prefer clear labels, deliberate spacing, and direct actions over decoration.

## Palette

- Soft neutral-gray background: `#f5f5f4`
- White surfaces: `#ffffff`
- Quiet secondary surface: `#f0f0ef`
- Near-black text and primary action: `#171717`
- Muted secondary text: `#666666`
- Neutral divider: `#dededc`

Use black and white for the workspace structure and actions. Keep success, warning, and error colors distinct and semantic so operational states remain recognizable.

## Typography

Use the existing Nunito sans serif for operational text and headings. Keep weight and spacing restrained.

## Layout and interaction

Design mobile as a first-class layout: reflow content, keep controls comfortable to tap, prevent horizontal overflow, and preserve existing quick navigation and POS workflows. Keep keyboard focus visible. The default theme is light monochrome; a dark monochrome theme is available as an option.

## Design dials

- Energy: 1
- Rhythm: 2
- Motion: 1

Use modest variation to make the most important action or metric easy to find. Keep animation limited to functional state changes and honor reduced-motion preferences.
