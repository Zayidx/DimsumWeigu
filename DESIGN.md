# Design direction

## Identity

The workspace is the operational side of the DimsumWeigu brand. Its interface should feel like a natural continuation of the public homepage, while keeping sales, inventory, and finance information easy to scan.

## Audience

Cashiers, outlet staff, and managers who use the workspace on phones as well as desktop screens.

## Personality

Simple, modern, warm, and practical. Keep the interface calm when showing dense operational data. Prefer clear labels, comfortable spacing, and direct actions over decoration.

## Palette

- Soft warm-gray background: `#f5f3ef`
- White surfaces: `#ffffff`
- Quiet secondary surface: `#efebe5`
- Cocoa text: `#342e29`
- Muted brown text: `#746b63`
- Muted clay action accent: `#805945`
- Muted clay hover: `#644331`
- Warm divider: `#e4ded7`

These colors keep the homepage's warm character while lowering saturation for long work sessions. Reserve the clay accent for primary actions, selected navigation, and key data emphasis. Keep success, warning, and error colors distinct and semantic.

## Typography

Use the existing Nunito sans serif for operational text. Use a restrained Georgia serif for page and section headings to echo the homepage's display typography.

## Layout and interaction

Design mobile as a first-class layout: reflow content, keep controls comfortable to tap, prevent horizontal overflow, and preserve the existing quick navigation and POS workflows. Keep keyboard focus visible and support both light and dark themes.

## Design dials

- Energy: 2
- Rhythm: 2
- Motion: 1

Use modest variation to make the most important action or metric easy to find. Keep animation limited to functional state changes and honor reduced-motion preferences.
